# Evidence checks, with sources

Checked 14 November 2026. These checks flag missing review evidence. They do not certify legal compliance, decide a retention period or authorise data sharing.

| Check | Source | What the command checks |
|---|---|---|
| purpose_missing | [NZ Privacy Act principle 1](https://www.privacy.org.nz/privacy-principles/1/) | Acquisition purpose is recorded. The operator decides whether each personal record is necessary and lawfully held. |
| access_review_due | [NZ principle 5](https://www.privacy.org.nz/privacy-principles/5/) and [Australian APP 11.1](https://www.oaic.gov.au/privacy/australian-privacy-principles/australian-privacy-principles-guidelines/chapter-11-app-11-security-of-personal-information) | A next access review date exists and has not passed. A date is evidence of scheduling, not proof that safeguards work. |
| retention_review_due | [NZ principle 9](https://www.privacy.org.nz/privacy-principles/9/) and [Australian APP 11.2](https://www.oaic.gov.au/privacy/australian-privacy-principles/australian-privacy-principles-guidelines/chapter-11-app-11-security-of-personal-information) | A next retention review date exists and has not passed. Review lawful retention requirements before removing anything. |
| accountable_owner_missing | Internal governance policy | Each acquisition has a responsible operator. This is not a statutory field requirement. |

The NZ principles address necessary collection, safeguards and retention. APP 11 applies where the Australian organisation is an APP entity. Its retention requirement has exceptions, including required legal retention. Get appropriate advice on applicability and acquisition-specific obligations.

Use /review-governance to record the actual purpose and the next review dates after a review, with a reason. Do not set dates just to clear flags. No automated deletion, employee transfer, regulatory filing or notification occurs here.

## Internal controls

Task completion requires evidence and finished prerequisites. Dependency cycles and links across acquisitions are rejected. Accepted benefits require an actual close date no later than today, a current or past period, evidence, a reviewer label different from the recorder, and completed linked work. Correcting a claim clears its approval. Reopened prerequisite work puts the benefit back on hold, including indirect prerequisites.

These are operating rules, not legislation. Actor labels are not authenticated identities. A shared installation must enforce identity and permissions separately. The 14-day stale-task rule is configurable operating policy, not a legal deadline.
